# Weekly review

[Procedures](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

Use this checklist once a week, or whenever work needs a reset. Record review notes in [Notes](../notes/README.md) if useful.

1. **Sort the [inbox](../inbox/README.md).** Move useful captures to notes, bookmarks, tasks, or another appropriate section. Remove each inbox copy after filing it.
2. **Refresh [tasks](../tasks/README.md).** Mark completed actions, choose what comes next, and check waiting items for follow-up. Include context or a follow-up date when useful.
3. **Check active [plans](../plans/README.md).** Update milestones and link concrete next actions to Tasks.
4. **Consolidate useful information.** Turn repeatable solutions into [procedures](README.md) and add takeaways to [learning](../learning/README.md).
5. **Retire stale material.** Move completed or superseded documents to the [archive](../archive/README.md) when worth keeping. Update indexes and links after moving files.

The review is complete when useful inbox items have a home and your next actions reflect current priorities.
